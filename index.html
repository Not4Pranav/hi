1. The Quadratic Steering Scrub Penalty (xU · u²)

In the Race2Win game engine (GameView.js / eW.integrate), lateral grip is strictly capped at latGrip = 24.0 m/s² for Formula A. When you turn the wheel harder than the centripetal grip limit (ω > latGrip / v), the engine does NOT turn the car tighter. Instead, it calculates the excess demand as a scrub factor u and bleeds your velocity:

v_new = max(0, v - (xU * u² + i) * dt)

Tapping arrow keys repeatedly triggers sudden spikes in u², shedding up to 1.5 - 2.5 seconds per lap. The secret to the perfect lap is smooth, minimum-angle steering that stays right at 90%-95% of the grip limit without crossing into the scrub zone.

2. The Hidden Keyboard "Auto-Gas-Cut" Penalty

By default, Race2Win applies an input assist profile to keyboard players (or.keyboard):

if (autoGasCut && slip > 0.15 && throttle > 0.4) { throttle = 0.40; }

If your rear tires slip by even 15% during a corner, the game automatically slashes your throttle to 40%! Gamepad players do not have this restriction. You can avoid this by either turning Drift Assist to "Off" in the in-game settings, or by running our Apex Pro Userscript which counteracts the auto-gas cut.

3. Authoritative 60Hz Physics Re-Simulation

Race2Win does not accept arbitrary claimed lap times from the browser. At the end of every official lap attempt, the client base64-encodes the tick-by-tick binary input stream (steer, throttle, brake, boost) and streams it to POST /api/attempts/:id/submit.

A worker thread on the server re-runs the physics simulation from tick 0 to tick N. Only laps whose inputs produce a valid trajectory without cutting track boundaries are verified and placed onto the leaderboard.
